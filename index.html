Questions Clients Ask

Can you fix color that another salon or a box dye left uneven?

Yes, that is our specialty. We assess your hair's history and current tone, then plan the correction so the result is balanced and healthy-looking.

Will my correction be finished in one visit?

It depends on the starting point. Some fixes are done in a single session, while others are safer in stages. We will tell you what to expect at your consultation.

Do you also do balayage and ombre?

Yes. We create balayage and ombre customized to your tone, cut and how much maintenance you want.
